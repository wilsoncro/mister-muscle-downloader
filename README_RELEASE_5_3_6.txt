Mister Muscle Downloader v5.3.6 - Independent Audio and Video Markers

- Video (V1): purple IN/OUT handles.
- Audio (A1): turquoise IN/OUT handles, editable independently.
- Use A IN / A OUT buttons, or drag directly over the audio waveform.
- Link A/V synchronizes markers. Dragging A1 automatically unlinks them.
- Video + Audio: independent A1/V1 ranges, video duration determines the final length.
- Video Only uses V1, Audio Only uses A1.
- Save A1 clips, split at playhead, clear, and export multiple independent audio clips.
- Full-length waveform and all previous stability fixes retained.
